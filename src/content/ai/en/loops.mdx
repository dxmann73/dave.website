## Loop engineering

Current trend that aims to string the a combination of loops together and let agents run the complete
cycle from feature/requirement to merge / production, with the loops running indepentently until
the desired result pops up. This is basically an attempt to remove the human in the loop.
Despite the fact that this incurs immense token cost, it is not clear how stable results are supposed
to emerge when the current process just isn't delivering them even with human intervention.
