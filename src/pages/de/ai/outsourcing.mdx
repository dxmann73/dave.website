# Outsourcing

Since we are effectively outsourcing to AI, what are first principles to make this work that
we already know.

## Orchestration

There is an orchestration tax you have to pay
https://addyosmani.com/blog/orchestration-tax

- Humans are the bottleneck
- Parallel work is a myth, you need context switching
- Parallel agents != shipped code
- https://addyosmani.com/blog/orchestration-tax

Guidelines:

- Scale fleet to review rate
- Sort the work: isolated work to delegate to background agents vs. complex tasks where the judgement is the work
- Batch your reviews. Review 4 agents in one sitting is much cheaper than checking one, leaving to do something else and returning cold
- Only spend the lock on judgement. Dont waste your brain on things the machine can verify itself.
- Protect your serial time. The bottleneck needs your best hours, not the leftover minutes between agent check-ins.
