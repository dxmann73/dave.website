# Engineering

As stated in the overview page, the goal is to communicate and ingest outsourced work efficiently.

On this page, we're going to look at what this means for engineering.

As is turns out, the principles remain very much the same, with added challenges and opportunities

## Hedging complexity

Complexity will emerge from the parts, as any rocket engineer will tell you. That's the reason why 100% test coverage is a myth:
You cannot possibly test any and all combinations with a reasonably complex system.
https://www.heise.de/hintergrund/Won-t-fix-Teil-2-Warum-sich-Bugs-nicht-systematisch-eliminieren-lassen-11308462.html

That means you are well advised to hedge complexity
- componentize / modularize: disentangle components, composition over inheritance
- reduce complexity of requirements => feeds back into the PDLC: "Let's do this another way"
- break down complex endeavours into easy steps

## Taste / Foresight

What gets ever more important is the question of [taste, or call it foresight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wm7tsiJ1nIo&t=872s). What does that mean?

Code that is written will have implications. For example: Do you fetch customer records, update them and write them back, or do you make an
effort to implement per-field changes on an atomic basis? No right or wrong here, but a lot of consequences down the road.

Decide for the deviosly simple approach, pile a lot of features on that which make assumptions on the underlying architecture,
and you may end up with a huge and risky refactor down the road.

Taste is about catching these issues before they arise. It can -from my experience- only really be acquired when you have been bitten by your own decisions.
That said, don't freeze, keep shipping, but be aware to correct course on a moment's notice.

## Make results verifiable

Wasserleitung abdrücken, Probefahrt, Bremsentest

## Constantly monitor behaviour of the system

Dehnungsmessstreifen, Begehungen, Sichtprüfung
Sensorik, Abwasserprüfung
Zusammenhang zwischen Feature goLive und Verhaltensänderungen
